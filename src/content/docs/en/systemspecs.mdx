In order to run Remote Incident Manager, your mobile device must meet or exceed the following requirements:

<Windows>
* iOS 26 or later
* A physical keyboard is recommended for the most optimal experience

</Windows>

<MacOS>
* Android 8.1.0 or later
* A physical keyboard is recommended for the most optimal experience

</MacOS>
